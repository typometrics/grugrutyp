Privacy and cookies

No tracking, no advertising, no third-party cookies. Here is everything the site keeps.

Cookie

namewhat forlifetime
grugrutyp_sessionkeeps you signed in to grugrutyp (Google, GitHub or ORCID sign-in). Set only when you sign in.30 days

It is necessary for the service you ask for, so under the EU ePrivacy rules it needs no consent; the notice at the bottom of the page is information, not a choice.

Stored in your browser, never sent to us

  • light or dark theme;
  • the last plot you made, so a reload restores it;
  • the width of the assistant panel, and colours you changed by hand;
  • that you have seen the cookie notice.

Clearing the site's data in your browser removes all of it.

On the server

  • Your account, if you create one: the name, e-mail address and picture your sign-in provider gives us, your saved queries, and how many assistant (LLM) requests you made today, for the daily quota.
  • Queries: the text of each query, how long it took and how many results it had, to find slow queries and bugs. Not who ran it.
  • Visits: the number of visits per day and section. The web server's access log holds IP addresses for 14 days, as every web server's does; the visit statistics are computed from it without keeping any IP address.
  • Assistant: what you type to the assistant is sent to OpenAI to answer it.
