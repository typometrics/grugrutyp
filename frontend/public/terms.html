grugrutyp — terms of service

grugrutyp is a free, non-commercial research tool for quantitative linguistic typology over the Universal Dependencies treebanks.

Use

The service is provided as is, without warranty of any kind, in the hope that it is useful for research and teaching. Numbers it reports are computed from third-party treebanks and, unless marked exact, may be computed on statistical samples — the interface marks sampled and imprecise values as such. Please cite responsibly and check the marked precision before publishing a number.

Do not attempt to disrupt the service, to access other users' data, or to use automated bulk querying that degrades it for others. Accounts of abusive users may be removed. Features that consume paid resources (such as LLM-assisted querying) are enabled per user at the operator's discretion.

Content and code

The underlying treebanks belong to the Universal Dependencies and SUD projects and carry their own licences. The grugrutyp software is open source under AGPL-3.0: github.com/typometrics/grugrutyp.
