grugrutyp — privacy policy

grugrutyp is a non-commercial research tool for quantitative linguistic typology, operated by Kim Gerdes. It shows no advertising and uses no third-party trackers or analytics.

Without an account

You can use the whole measuring and search interface without signing in. Queries you run are logged — their text, timing and result size, to improve the tool and find slow query shapes — but never who asked: no IP address, no account, no browser fingerprint is attached to the log, and entries are deleted after 180 days. Plot customisations (colours, markers, group names) are stored in your own browser (localStorage) and never sent to the server.

With an account

Signing in uses OAuth via Google, GitHub or ORCID. grugrutyp never sees or stores a password. What we store is what the provider vouches for: a stable identifier, your display name, your email address if the provider shares one, and your avatar URL. We use it to show you your account and to let administrators manage access; we never share or sell it. Saved queries are stored under your account until you delete them. The session is one signed cookie holding only your account id; there are no other cookies.

Your data

To see or delete what is stored about you, write to kim@gerdes.fr. Deleting an account removes the account row and its saved queries. The software is open source (github.com/typometrics/grugrutyp, AGPL-3.0), so everything described here can be checked in the code.
